PROJECT: Vernox AI — UI/UX Overhaul (Dashboard + Landing Page)

SCOPE: Make the product look intentional, professional, and purpose-built — not AI-generated.

CURRENT PROBLEM:
- Landing page looks like it was generated by an AI tool (generic gradients, too many colors, rounded everything, stock imagery)
- Dashboard is functional but lacks visual hierarchy and personality
- User portal doesn't feel like a professional SaaS — feels generic
- No clear brand identity

DESIGN PRINCIPLES (READ CAREFULLY):
1. **Avoid AI aesthetic:** No gradients that fade, no blur effects, no 6+ colors, no generic stock photos
2. **Intentional & sparse:** Every design choice serves a purpose. Empty space is okay.
3. **Hierarchy is clear:** Headings, subheadings, body text sizes are distinct. Users know where to look.
4. **Consistent typography:** Max 2 font families (e.g., Inter for UI + one serif for headlines)
5. **Color palette:** Max 5 colors (primary, secondary, neutral, accent, alert). Stick to it.
6. **No heavy animation:** Subtle transitions only (200ms fade, 300ms slide). No bouncing, no floating.

INSPIRATION (do NOT copy, but study these):
- Stripe.com (minimal, professional, purposeful)
- Linear.app (clean, dark mode, clear hierarchy)
- Vercel.com (spacious, typography-driven)
- Notion.so (simple cards, dark mode, functional)

TASK 1: COLOR PALETTE
Define a professional, minimal color system:

Primary: #1a1a1a or #0f0f0f (dark, neutral, trustworthy)
Secondary: #4f46e5 or #6366f1 (indigo — accent, call-to-action)
Neutral: #6b7280 (medium gray for secondary text)
Alert: #dc2626 (red for errors, warnings)
Success: #16a34a (green for confirmations)
Background: #ffffff (light) or #0f0f0f (dark, if dark mode)
Border: #e5e7eb (very light gray)

Choose ONE of these as your brand. Stick to it everywhere.

TASK 2: TYPOGRAPHY
Use ONLY these fonts:

Headlines (h1, h2, h3): Inter Bold or Inter SemiBold, letter-spacing: -0.02em
Body text: Inter Regular, line-height: 1.6, letter-spacing: 0
Monospace (code): JetBrains Mono or Courier New

Font sizes:
- h1 (page title): 32px / 2rem
- h2 (section title): 24px / 1.5rem
- h3 (subsection): 18px / 1.125rem
- body: 14px / 0.875rem (UI), 16px / 1rem (prose)
- small (secondary text): 12px / 0.75rem

Current: tabs in sidebar, generic cards, no hierarchy
New approach: professional SaaS dashboard

Sidebar:
- Dark background (#1a1a1a), white text

Tables:

- Padding: 12px per cell

Stats cards (top of dashboard):
- 4 cards in a grid: Total, New, Converted, Rate

Toggle in settings: one simple toggle switch

TASK 6: REMOVE AI AESTHETIC
Delete these immediately:
- ❌ Gradient backgrounds (linear-gradient, radial-gradient)
- ❌ Blur effects (backdrop-filter: blur)
- ❌ Glowing text / box-shadow: 0 0 50px
- ❌ Overly smooth animations (cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) — use ease-in-out instead)
- ❌ Floating/floating-up animations
- ❌ 8+ colors in use
- ❌ Stock photos of people smiling
- ❌ Confetti, fireworks, particle effects
- ❌ Too many rounded corners (max 8px, use sharp corners 0px where appropriate)
- ❌ Every icon being different (use one icon library: Feather Icons or Heroicons)

Replace with:
- ✅ Solid colors (use your 5-color palette only)
- ✅ Simple borders (1px solid)
- ✅ Subtle transitions (opacity 200ms, transform 300ms)
- ✅ Clear typography
- ✅ Lots of whitespace
- ✅ Consistent spacing (8px grid: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48px)
- ✅ One icon library throughout
